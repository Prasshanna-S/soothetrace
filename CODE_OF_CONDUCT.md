# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and safe for people with different backgrounds and experiences.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and open to good-faith feedback.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the sensitivity of family audio and health-adjacent information.
- Discuss technical and product claims with evidence and appropriate uncertainty.
- Welcome questions from contributors with different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sharing another person's recordings, personal data, credentials, or private communications without permission.
- Misrepresenting prototype results as medical, clinical, or population-validated claims.
- Pressuring anyone to provide recordings or disclose sensitive information.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the hosting platform. Do not include sensitive recordings or credentials in a public issue. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other appropriate action.
