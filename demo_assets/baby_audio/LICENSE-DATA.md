# Baby Audio Data Notice

The files in this directory contain audio from
[Donate-a-Cry](https://github.com/gveres/donateacry-corpus), pinned for provenance at commit
[`6baf5aca0460964ad072653585837f968a87c2af`](https://github.com/gveres/donateacry-corpus/tree/6baf5aca0460964ad072653585837f968a87c2af).

The source repository states that the database is made available under the
[Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) and that rights in
individual contents are licensed under the
[Database Contents License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/).

The cleaned WAV derivative is credited by the source repository to Arra’di Nur Rizal, Jonathan Jan,
Luise Dürlich, and Shifei Chen as part of a KTH Royal Institute of Technology course project. Their
cleaned corpus converted the source recordings to 8 kHz WAV, organized contributor-supplied labels,
and manually removed recordings judged not to contain infant cries.

`Baby 1`, `Baby 2`, and `Baby 3` are local demonstration labels. Each folder shares one app-install
UUID. The source documentation explicitly says that this UUID identifies an installed app instance,
not a device, user, caregiver, or verified baby. The grouping is therefore a demo proxy and must not
be described as confirmed infant identity.

The source licenses do not by themselves establish consent for every future biometric, privacy, or
personality-right use. This repository includes the clips as research and hackathon demonstration
fixtures. It does not claim that they are suitable medical, production, or commercial identity
data.
